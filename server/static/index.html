03 — METHOD

Ladders, not black boxes.

Each entity type climbs a public ladder from the exact value to a placeholder. The rung is chosen per entity type from a learned depth table; the ladder itself is assumed known to the attacker. What you see here is the whole codebook.

A middle rung can be more identifying than a placeholder — an unusual attribute combination may be unique in the registry. That is why re-identification, not span survival, is the headline metric.
